# Stil-Test: 4 komplett verschiedene Richtungen

Alle 4 in Flow laufen lassen → nebeneinander anschauen → einen Stil wählen.
Problem bei Version 1: zu „Pixar-Mensch“, wirkt komisch/generisch.
Ziel: **einfach, ikonisch, sofort erkennbar** – wie ein Logo, nicht wie ein Filmcharakter.

---

## A) Emoji-Maskottchen (simpel, wie ein Sticker)
```
Cute minimalist 3D emoji-style mascot, a small round detective character with no visible face, only a black fedora hat with orange band and two glowing orange round glasses floating in a pitch black face. Wearing a dark trench coat with upturned collar. Glossy soft 3D render like an app icon or sticker, very simple shapes, chunky proportions, big head small body, centered, plain dark background, iconic and readable at small size.
```

## B) Noir-Schatten (flach, grafisch, sehr ikonisch)
```
Flat vector illustration, film noir style, silhouette of a mysterious detective, face completely in black shadow, only two small glowing orange circles where the glasses are, black fedora hat, long trench coat with upturned collar, bold graphic shapes, limited color palette black dark grey and bright orange, strong contrast, clean poster design, plain background, iconic logo-like character.
```

## C) Street-Detektiv (jung, modern, cooler Vibe)
```
Stylized 3D character, young anonymous street detective, black bucket hat pulled low, face hidden behind a black face mask with a glowing orange visor display showing two simple pixel eyes, oversized black techwear jacket with high collar, orange details, holding a phone with a magnifying glass icon, cool confident pose, Fortnite-like stylized render, plain dark background, half body.
```

## D) Spielzeug-Figur (Vinyl-Toy, sammelbar)
```
Designer vinyl toy figure of a detective, matte plastic material, big head small body, smooth faceless black head with two round orange glowing glasses, tiny black fedora with orange band, mini trench coat, holding a tiny magnifying glass, product photo on a plain dark grey background, soft studio lighting, collectible art toy style.
```
